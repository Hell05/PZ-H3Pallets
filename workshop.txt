title=H3 Pallets
description=Stack items on pallets! You can now interact with the empty pallets and stack 91 different items on them!
description=
description=Pallets accept partially filled sacks, propane tanks and damaged or worn tires and will return them in the same condition when taken.
description=You can also pickup the pallets with items on them (unless they are too heavy).
description=
description=Use existing pallets in the world or build your own from the building menu Storage category.
description=Vanilla pallets have their values updated so they match the displayed quantity.
description=Empty pallets will list all categories and items compatible.
description=Pallets with items on them only display relevant options.
description=
description=Mix and match, some pallets accept multiple different items e.g. Ammo Cartons.
description=
description=Every stage has a visual change in quantity!
description=Add or Remove in steps or larger quantities in one go.
description=
description=Safe to add mid save (pallets that have already been loaded will still show the vanilla take choice but once you interact it goes away).
description=Since this mod adds tiles removing it mid save is not recommended! (You need to remove all pallet tiles from the world before doing so).
description=Fully MP compatible! Server authoritative logic that has been tested on a dedicated server.
description=Unlikely to be compatible with other Pallet mods that rely on or modify the vanilla ISTakeBricks TimedAction.
description=
